Who We Are

Students Islamic Organisation of India (SIO) is an ideological organization that has worked toward the social advancement and development of the student community since its founding on October 19, 1982.

As a dynamic, nationwide movement of students and youth, we are committed to fostering moral excellence, academic brilliance, and social responsibility. Our ultimate endeavor is to prepare the youth to become proactive agents of change, working toward the reconstruction of society in the light of Divine guidance.

Mission & Vision

Our mission

Empowering Youth, Rebuilding Society

Driven by a profound sense of purpose, we pour our hearts into the youth—igniting their minds, deepening their spiritual strength, and grounding them in Islamic moral values. On campuses, we champion inclusive, value-driven education that leaves no student behind. Through community service, disaster relief, and human rights advocacy, we stand with the marginalized, striving every day for a meaningful reconstruction of society.

Our vision

The Future We Are Building

We dream of a world where no student is left behind, where the energy of youth is a force for ultimate good, and where society is beautifully transformed through righteousness, equality, and compassion.

Our History

  1. 1982 – The Beginning

    Born from a vision to nurture a generation of knowledgeable, morally conscious and socially responsible students, the Students Islamic Organisation of India (SIO) was established on 19 October 1982. It began a journey dedicated to preparing students and youth for the reconstruction of society in the light of Divine Guidance.

  2. The Foundation – A Student Movement

    SIO emerged as a platform to bring students together through knowledge, Islamic values, intellectual development and constructive engagement. Its vision was to make education a means of building character, strengthening conscience and creating positive change in society.

  3. A Vision Reaching Campuses Across India

    Over the years, SIO expanded its presence across India, reaching students from different regions and educational backgrounds. Through educational initiatives, student engagement, awareness programmes and social activities, the organisation continued to strengthen its commitment to the student community.

  4. Today – Continuing the Mission

    Today, SIO continues its journey of nurturing enlightened, responsible and socially conscious students. With a commitment to education, moral values, leadership and service, it strives to inspire young minds to become a force for positive change in society.

Achievements

  • Guinness World Record – Largest Recycling Lesson (2015)

    SIO Coimbatore participated in a Guinness World Record event organised by Coimbatore City Municipal Corporation, with 12,994 participants in the world's largest recycling lesson on 5 August 2015.

  • ACC ECO – The Real Heroes Award (2015)

    Recognised with the ACC ECO Real Heroes Award for outstanding contributions towards Chennai and Cuddalore flood relief efforts in 2015.

  • Nationwide COVID-19 Relief Initiative (2020)

    During the COVID-19 lockdown, SIO volunteers across India supported stranded students, migrant workers and vulnerable families through food distribution, ration kits, medical assistance and temporary shelter.
