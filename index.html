Who We Are

Students Islamic Organisation of India (SIO) is an ideological organization that has worked toward the social advancement and development of the student community since its founding on October 19, 1982.

As a dynamic, nationwide movement of students and youth, we are committed to fostering moral excellence, academic brilliance, and social responsibility. Our ultimate endeavor is to prepare the youth to become proactive agents of change, working toward the reconstruction of society in the light of Divine guidance.

Mission & Vision

Our mission

Build capable, caring students

To help students grow in knowledge, character and responsibility, and to give them real opportunities to serve their communities.

Our vision

A generation that leads with integrity

A student community that studies seriously, acts justly and contributes to a peaceful and inclusive society.
